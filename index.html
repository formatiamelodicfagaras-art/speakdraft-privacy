Overview

SpeakDraft is a voice recording and transcription application. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

  • Audio recordings — stored locally on your device only. We do not upload your recordings to our servers.
  • Transcription text — generated from your recordings and stored locally on your device.
  • OpenAI API key — stored securely in your device's encrypted keychain. Never transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

SpeakDraft uses the OpenAI Whisper API to transcribe your audio. When you record and transcribe, your audio is sent to OpenAI's servers for processing. Please review OpenAI's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle audio data.

No other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.

Data Storage

  • All recordings and transcripts are stored locally on your device.
  • No data is stored on our servers — we have none.
  • Deleting the app removes all associated data from your device.

Microphone Access

SpeakDraft requires microphone access solely to record your voice. Recordings are never accessed without your explicit action.

Children's Privacy

SpeakDraft is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
